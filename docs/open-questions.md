# Open questions

Anything without adequate source or experimental evidence stays here. `HYPOTHESIS` is not promoted to `CONFIRMED` by consensus alone.

| ID | Question | Why it matters | Current hypothesis | Validation / exit condition | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OQ-001 | Which initial product workload anchors the design? | “General memory” makes selection and evaluation underspecified. | Personal/project assistant conversations are the first workload; the platform contract remains domain-neutral. | Freeze a representative workload and acceptance cases before MVP coding. | OPEN |
| OQ-002 | What is the acceptable false-positive rate for `shouldRemember`? | A bad long-lived write may cost more than a missed low-value preference. | Optimize thresholds by memory type and downstream risk, not one global score. Feature 2 validates the deterministic policy mechanism against 17 frozen/dev fixture cases, not real-model quality or calibrated thresholds. | Label a representative real-model write-policy dataset and publish precision/recall by class. | OPEN |
| OQ-003 | How are event time, valid time, and uncertain natural-language dates normalized? | Temporal answers and supersession depend on more than `created_at`. | Feature 3 now preserves original text, half-open parsed bounds, precision, and confidence and safely conflicts when ordering is unprovable. Natural-language parser quality and relative-date anchoring remain unvalidated. | Real extraction/parser suite covering exact, partial, relative, locale-specific, and conflicting dates. | OPEN |
| OQ-004 | When should a new assertion supersede, coexist with, or conflict with an old version? | Destructive overwrite loses history; universal versioning creates pollution. | Feature 3 implements deterministic scope+subject+predicate identity, explicit `SINGLE`/`SET` cardinality, temporal-overlap rules, replay, correction, and invalidation. The 14-case fixture and PostgreSQL invariants validate the mechanism, not real-world classification quality. | Held-out contradiction benchmark and error analysis for lineage/cardinality classification. | OPEN |
| OQ-005 | What information is safe to persist automatically? | PII and prompt-injected content create durable risk. | Deny high-risk classes by default; attach trust/provenance and require explicit policy for sensitive memories. Feature 2 proves model labels cannot downgrade deterministic detection. Feature 5 adds verified authorization and a structural untrusted-data/escaping fixture, but does not establish real-model poisoning resistance. | Threat model, classifier evaluation, and Feature 6 write-to-use red-team cases against fixed model snapshots. | OPEN |
| OQ-006 | Does built-in PostgreSQL FTS + pgvector meet quality and latency targets? | It determines whether OpenSearch or a dedicated vector store is justified. | Feature 4 implements the PostgreSQL mechanism and a synthetic conformance fixture; representative-corpus quality/latency remains unmeasured. | Compare Recall@K and p95 latency at representative corpus sizes. | OPEN |
| OQ-007 | RRF, learned fusion, or calibrated weighted scoring? | Raw scores from different retrievers are not naturally comparable. | Feature 4 implements configurable RRF and records component ranks. Its synthetic fixture validates mechanics, not weight selection. | Dev/test split ablation with statistical uncertainty and latency/cost. | OPEN |
| OQ-008 | Is an LLM reranker worth its cost? | It may improve reasoning-heavy recall but dominates latency and tokens. | Feature 4 adds an opt-in typed reranker with deadline/identity fallback; no real reranker is selected. | Quality/cost Pareto comparison against no reranker. | OPEN |
| OQ-009 | What is the user-visible consistency contract after a message? | Async extraction improves latency but creates a window where a new fact is not retrievable. | Feature 4 exposes a projection transition watermark and zero-row invalidation checkpoint. Its runtime smoke confirms the mechanism and restart behavior, not a representative freshness distribution. The final freshness SLO and synchronous read-your-write option remain undecided. | Measure representative freshness distributions and compare asynchronous versus explicit read-your-write behavior. | OPEN |
| OQ-010 | What does “forget” mean for historical versions and audit logs? | Privacy deletion and evidentiary audit can conflict; even a raw hash of low-entropy PII may disclose content by guessing. | Feature 5 implements PostgreSQL request-time hiding plus fenced purge of payloads, derived content/fingerprints, current/search projections, and affected jobs; it retains append-only content-safe audit facts and opaque UUID tombstones. Dead operations stay isolated and require privacy-admin requeue. Remote run #22 confirms PostgreSQL propagation, replay, and concurrency mechanics. Scope/source/session/idempotency identifiers are retained under an opaque-ID contract; backup/WAL/replica/provider scope and legal retention remain unestablished. | Legal/product policy for identifier content plus key, backup, replica, and provider lifecycle input for the deployment scope. | OPEN |
| OQ-011 | Is an entity/relationship graph needed in the first release? | Graphs help multi-hop and temporal relations but add dual-write and query complexity. | Postgres relations/JSON metadata first; graph projection deferred. | Entity/multi-hop benchmark shows material gain beyond relational and hybrid retrieval. | OPEN |
| OQ-012 | Which LLM and embedding snapshots make the benchmark reproducible and affordable? | Unpinned hosted models drift and invalidate comparisons. | Adapters plus run manifests; include at least one reproducible local or version-pinned path where practical. | Provider/version/cost matrix and replayable smoke dataset. | OPEN |
| OQ-013 | Which external benchmark licenses permit redistribution or derived fixtures? | Reproducibility cannot violate dataset terms. | Fetch official datasets in setup scripts rather than vendoring by default. | Record license and redistribution status for LoCoMo, LongMemEval, and BEAM. | OPEN |
| OQ-014 | What are the initial SLOs and scale envelope? | “Millions of users” is not a useful design input without workload and retention assumptions. | Derive targets from three explicit load profiles before optimization. | Capacity model with QPS, memories/user, write amplification, retention, and cost. | OPEN |
| OQ-015 | How should multiple agents share memory safely? | Shared stores risk information leakage and concurrent conflicting writes. | Tenant/user/agent scopes plus ACLs and optimistic version checks; no implicit global sharing. | Authorization matrix, concurrency tests, and multi-agent benchmark cases. | OPEN |
