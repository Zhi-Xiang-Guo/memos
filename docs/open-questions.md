# Open questions

Anything without adequate source or experimental evidence stays here. `HYPOTHESIS` is not promoted to `CONFIRMED` by consensus alone.

| ID | Question | Why it matters | Current hypothesis | Validation / exit condition | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| OQ-001 | Which initial product workload anchors the design? | “General memory” makes selection and evaluation underspecified. | Personal/project assistant conversations are the first workload; the platform contract remains domain-neutral. | Freeze a representative workload and acceptance cases before MVP coding. | OPEN |
| OQ-002 | What is the acceptable false-positive rate for `shouldRemember`? | A bad long-lived write may cost more than a missed low-value preference. | Optimize thresholds by memory type and downstream risk, not one global score. Feature 2 validates the deterministic policy mechanism against 17 frozen/dev fixture cases, not real-model quality or calibrated thresholds. | Label a representative real-model write-policy dataset and publish precision/recall by class. | OPEN |
| OQ-003 | How are event time, valid time, and uncertain natural-language dates normalized? | Temporal answers and supersession depend on more than `created_at`. | Store parsed intervals plus original expression and parser confidence; abstain on ambiguity. | Temporal test suite covering exact, partial, relative, and conflicting dates. | OPEN |
| OQ-004 | When should a new assertion supersede, coexist with, or conflict with an old version? | Destructive overwrite loses history; universal versioning creates pollution. | Use entity + predicate + scope + temporal overlap, with confidence-aware rules and review state. | Contradiction benchmark and invariants for `CURRENT/HISTORICAL/CONFLICTED/INVALIDATED`. | OPEN |
| OQ-005 | What information is safe to persist automatically? | PII and prompt-injected content create durable risk. | Deny high-risk classes by default; attach trust/provenance and require explicit policy for sensitive memories. Feature 2 now proves model labels cannot downgrade deterministic detection and rejected/review candidates persist without proposal content. | Threat model, classifier evaluation, Feature 5 deletion proof, and red-team memory-injection cases. | OPEN |
| OQ-006 | Does built-in PostgreSQL FTS + pgvector meet quality and latency targets? | It determines whether OpenSearch or a dedicated vector store is justified. | It is sufficient for MVP and moderate scale. | Compare Recall@K and p95 latency at representative corpus sizes. | OPEN |
| OQ-007 | RRF, learned fusion, or calibrated weighted scoring? | Raw scores from different retrievers are not naturally comparable. | Start with RRF; introduce calibrated/learned fusion only after labeled data exists. | Dev/test split ablation with statistical uncertainty and latency/cost. | OPEN |
| OQ-008 | Is an LLM reranker worth its cost? | It may improve reasoning-heavy recall but dominates latency and tokens. | Cross-encoder or LLM reranking is opt-in after cheap filters; no default until measured. | Quality/cost Pareto comparison against no reranker. | OPEN |
| OQ-009 | What is the user-visible consistency contract after a message? | Async extraction improves latency but creates a window where a new fact is not retrievable. | Feature 1 returns an ingestion receipt and exposes durable job status; Feature 2 atomically records extraction completion plus a still-visible downstream pending intent. Explicit synchronous writes remain undecided. | End-to-end version/projection freshness tests and API contract review. Features 1–2 do not yet prove queryability. | OPEN |
| OQ-010 | What does “forget” mean for historical versions and audit logs? | Privacy deletion and evidentiary audit can conflict; even a raw hash of low-entropy PII may disclose content by guessing. | Crypto-shred or purge payloads and content-derived fingerprints while retaining minimal non-content audit tombstones based on opaque IDs/generation state, subject to policy. | Legal/product policy and key-lifecycle input; deletion propagation and dictionary-guess test across every projection. | OPEN |
| OQ-011 | Is an entity/relationship graph needed in the first release? | Graphs help multi-hop and temporal relations but add dual-write and query complexity. | Postgres relations/JSON metadata first; graph projection deferred. | Entity/multi-hop benchmark shows material gain beyond relational and hybrid retrieval. | OPEN |
| OQ-012 | Which LLM and embedding snapshots make the benchmark reproducible and affordable? | Unpinned hosted models drift and invalidate comparisons. | Adapters plus run manifests; include at least one reproducible local or version-pinned path where practical. | Provider/version/cost matrix and replayable smoke dataset. | OPEN |
| OQ-013 | Which external benchmark licenses permit redistribution or derived fixtures? | Reproducibility cannot violate dataset terms. | Fetch official datasets in setup scripts rather than vendoring by default. | Record license and redistribution status for LoCoMo, LongMemEval, and BEAM. | OPEN |
| OQ-014 | What are the initial SLOs and scale envelope? | “Millions of users” is not a useful design input without workload and retention assumptions. | Derive targets from three explicit load profiles before optimization. | Capacity model with QPS, memories/user, write amplification, retention, and cost. | OPEN |
| OQ-015 | How should multiple agents share memory safely? | Shared stores risk information leakage and concurrent conflicting writes. | Tenant/user/agent scopes plus ACLs and optimistic version checks; no implicit global sharing. | Authorization matrix, concurrency tests, and multi-agent benchmark cases. | OPEN |
